# cpp/include/uneye/ — headers
`bitcn.hpp` bidirectional 2-channel TCN (`features2`, `BiTCN`, `detect`); `causal_engine.hpp` strictly causal streaming TCN; `engine.hpp` U-Net back-ends (native / ONNX); `detector.hpp` online labeling with U'n'Eye;
`gaze_engine.hpp` deterministic streaming gaze-state engine; `safety_guard.hpp` final guard (states, rules, limits and their source); `gaze_tables.hpp` GENERATED by `cpp/scripts/fit_landing_tables.py` (do not edit);
`simulator.hpp` synthetic gaze with labels; `spsc_queue.hpp` lock-free tracker-thread -> engine queue.
